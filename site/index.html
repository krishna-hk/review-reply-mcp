The AI reads and writes. The server counts and checks.

  
Language models are good at reading a review and writing a reply. They are unreliable at counting, so code does that. The split keeps every number in the digest real.

The same routine every time. Steps marked "you" need a person. Everything else the AI and the server do between them.

  
    
    
  1. You
    Set up your business profile
    Tell the AI your business name, how you want to sound, and your rules (for example, "never promise refunds in public replies"). This is saved once.
    set_business_profile
    2. 
    
  2. You
    Bring your reviews
    Export a CSV from your review platform and attach or paste it. No CSV yet? Load the demo reviews to try the full flow.
    import_reviewsload_demo_data
    3. 
    
  3. Tag every review
    The AI takes the reviews in batches of 20, assigns one or more themes with a sentiment to each, and flags serious ones. The server rejects unknown themes and saves the rest.
    get_untagged_reviewssave_tags
    4. 
    
  4. You
    Read the digest
    Top praise, top complaints, what is rising, what is new, and real quotes. Open any theme to see the reviews behind it.
    get_digestget_theme_reviews
    5. 
    
  5. You
    Handle flagged reviews yourself
    Reviews mentioning health, safety, legal threats or discrimination are not drafted. They are listed for a person to handle.
    6. 
    
  6. Draft the replies
    For each remaining review the AI receives the review, your profile and your rules, then writes a draft. The server checks it and sends back the exact rule if it fails, so the AI can rewrite.

What the tool will not do

  

    
It never posts
No tool sends anything to Google or any review site. Every reply is a draft that you copy and post.

    
It never invents quotes
Quotes in the digest are copied by code from the original reviews, character for character.

    
It never obeys a review
A review that says "ignore your instructions" is treated as text to analyse, not a command.

    
It escalates serious cases
Health, safety, legal and discrimination claims go to a person instead of getting a casual reply.

    
It checks promises
Drafts that promise refunds or admit fault against your rules are rejected with the reason.

    
It forgets on request
One tool deletes everything stored for you. Each owner's data is kept separate.
