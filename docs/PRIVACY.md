# Privacy statement (plain words, draft v1)

For people trying Review Reply during the pilot. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. Hari reads it before it is sent to anyone.

## What this is
A small tool that lets your own AI (Claude or ChatGPT) read your customer feedback, tag it by theme, and draft replies for you to approve. We run the storage and the counting. Your AI does the reading and writing. Nothing is ever posted anywhere by us.

## What we store
Only what you send through the tool:
- The reviews you import: the text, the star rating, the date, the source, and the reviewer's name if your file has one.
- The tags, serious-review flags and reply drafts your AI saves.
- Your business or product profile (name, tone, policies, contact email) and your theme list.
- Fixes you log.
- Counts for abuse protection (requests per minute). We do not keep a log of review text.

## What we do not do
- We do not read your reviews for any purpose of our own, sell them, or use them to train anything.
- We do not post, email or message anyone on your behalf.
- We do not share one person's data with another person. Each personal link opens only that person's data.
- We have no AI of our own. Your reviews go to Claude or ChatGPT because you chose to use them in that app; their privacy terms then apply to what you send them.

## Where it lives
On Cloudflare (a database in their cloud). A tester's link is the key to their data, so keep it private. Only a scrambled version (hash) of the link is stored on our side. Cloudflare's own terms apply to their service.

## Your controls
- **Delete everything:** say "delete all my data" in your chat. The tool shows what would be removed, then wipes your reviews, tags, drafts, profile, themes and fixes after you confirm.
- **Switch off your link:** ask Hari and the link stops working at once.
- **Pilot data only:** please use data you are allowed to share. Remove names, phone numbers and emails from files if you can. Do not import anything you would not want stored in a small test system.

## Reviews are treated as untrusted
Text inside a review is only data to analyse. If a review says "ignore your instructions", it is flagged as spam and ignored.

## Limits of this pilot
This is a test, not a finished service. There is no uptime promise. Free-plan limits apply (verify at deploy time). If something goes wrong with your data, tell Hari at the email you were sent the link from.
