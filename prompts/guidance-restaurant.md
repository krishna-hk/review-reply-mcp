Examples: "quiet corner" is Atmosphere and comfort positive; "arrived on time" is Wait time and speed positive; "Delicious" alone is Product or food quality positive.

- Order mistakes (missing, wrong or forgotten items, wrong address on a label) are **Booking and ordering** negative. Staff attitude is only for manner: rude, dismissive, ignoring people, kind, attentive.
- Couriers and riders: rudeness, wrong door, food left outside, a leaking bag, cold food on arrival all go under **Delivery and packaging**. Late delivery is also Wait time and speed. Staff attitude is only for people inside the venue.
- Waiting to be seated, served or paid is **Wait time and speed** negative. Staff ignoring or snapping at people is **Staff attitude** negative. Tag both if the review says both. "Service" can mean either; slowness is Wait time, friendliness or rudeness is Staff attitude.
- Staff arguing or shouting in front of customers is Staff attitude negative.
- A problem the review says keeps happening ("wrong order again", "second time", "last 3 visits") gets the theme it is about **and** Consistency negative. A swing between good and bad ("great last time, poor this time") is Consistency negative. "Same great food every time" is Consistency positive.
- "Called twice" is not Communication and follow-up unless the review says nobody answered or replied.
- Food temperature (hot, warm, cold, lukewarm) is Product or food quality, even for delivery orders. "Rushed" or "hurried" service counts under Wait time and speed.
- Praise or criticism of "service" that is about how people behaved ("great service") is Staff attitude. Slowness is Wait time and speed.
- Hair, insects or other foreign objects in food are Cleanliness negative (and are serious, see below). A taste or cooking complaint is Product or food quality.
- For delivery orders, food that arrives cold, soggy or damaged is Product or food quality negative **and** Delivery and packaging negative. Praise for the rider in the same review does not cancel it: use one overall sentiment per theme.
- An order that never arrives or arrives with items missing is Booking and ordering negative. Add Delivery and packaging negative only if the review blames the rider, the handover or the packaging.
- Hot or warm food on a delivery order is Product or food quality positive and Delivery and packaging positive. Cold or soggy is negative for both.
- A dish with an ingredient the customer said to avoid, or ignored dietary requests, is Menu and dietary needs negative; add Staff attitude negative if they say staff were told and ignored it. A plain mix-up with no dietary issue is Booking and ordering.
- Money disputes (double charge, deposit or refund not returned) are not Price and value. Tag what went wrong instead: Booking and ordering for a cancelled booking, Communication and follow-up if nobody replied, Staff attitude if staff were dismissive. Price and value is only about cost compared with what the customer got.
- Behaviour in front of customers that spoils the visit ("ruined our night") is Staff attitude negative and Atmosphere and comfort negative.
