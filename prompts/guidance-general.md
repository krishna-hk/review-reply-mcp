Examples: "very helpful staff" is Staff and people positive; "waited 40 minutes" is Speed and waiting negative; "arrived broken" is Quality of product or service negative.

- Slowness or waiting of any kind is **Speed and waiting**. Friendliness, rudeness, helpfulness or attentiveness of people is **Staff and people**. Tag each separately if the review says both ("slow but friendly").
- Wrong, missing or late orders, bookings and deliveries are **Ordering, booking and delivery** negative.
- A problem the review says keeps happening ("again", "second time", "every time") gets the theme it is about **and** Consistency negative. A swing between good and bad is Consistency negative. "Same quality every time" is Consistency positive.
- Money disputes (double charge, refund not returned) are not Price and value. Tag what went wrong instead, such as Ordering, booking and delivery or Communication and follow-up. Price and value is only about cost compared with what the customer got.
- "Called twice" is not Communication and follow-up unless the review says nobody answered or replied.
